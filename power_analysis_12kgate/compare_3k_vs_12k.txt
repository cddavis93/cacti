VERDICT:
  NCC hardware power/energy itself is exactly 4.00x higher at 12000 gates
  (185.54 mW -> 742.17 mW), a deterministic consequence of
  FMA_GATE_COUNT appearing linearly in ncc.cc's area/dynamic/leakage formulas.

  But NCC hardware is a small fraction of total system power (L1+L2 SRAM dominates):
    ncc_fp3 config: NCC share averages 0.961% at 3K gates, 3.735% at 12K gates
    mixed   config: NCC share averages 0.414% at 3K gates, 1.637% at 12K gates

  Net effect on TOTAL system power (full_total_W):
    ncc_fp3 config: average +2.897% change, 3K -> 12K gates
    mixed   config: average +2.086% change, 3K -> 12K gates

  => NOT SIGNIFICANT at the system level. Quadrupling the gate-count assumption
     (3000 -> 12000) changes total system power/energy by well under 5%, because
     NCC hardware is a small slice of a total dominated by L1/L2 SRAM leakage and
     dynamic access energy, which do not depend on FMA_GATE_COUNT at all.
